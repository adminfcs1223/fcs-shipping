Daily pick up sheet

One sheet per day, same columns as the clipboard. "→ Pickup sheet" on any quote drops it here; the ✓ crosses a pickup off for everyone at once.

Open pickup times

Add the times you're available — customers book them on the website when they lock in a quote. A chosen time is held for 15 minutes while they finish.
