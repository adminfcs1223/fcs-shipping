Ship it your way

Three easy ways to get it home

However your things reach our Brooklyn warehouse, they leave on the next Thursday sailing.

Mail it straight to us

Shopping online? Put our warehouse down as your delivery address — 9502 Ditmas Ave, Building 4, Brooklyn NY 11236 — and your Amazon, Walmart, or SHEIN orders come straight to us. We sign for them, keep them safe, and load them on the next vessel. No box is too small; a single package is enough to start.

Pack your own barrel

Buy an empty barrel or bin right from us, take it home, or pack it at the warehouse — Monday to Friday, 9 to 5. Bring the rice, the oil, the school clothes, the little luxuries; take your time and pack it your way. When the lid goes on, we band it, label it, and it's manifested for the island the same day.

Or let our hands do it

Been doing this since 2017 — nobody packs a barrel tighter. Drop your things off loose or request a pickup, and our team packs, seals, and manifests everything for you. You get a tracking number on the spot and a call the moment it lands in St. Lucia.
